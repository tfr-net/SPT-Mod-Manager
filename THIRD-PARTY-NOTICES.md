# Third-party notices

## 7-Zip (7zr.exe)

The Windows build of SPT Mod Manager includes `7zr.exe`, the 7-Zip Reduced Standalone Console by Igor Pavlov
(https://www.7-zip.org, source at https://github.com/ip7z/7zip), used unmodified to unpack .7z archives when 7-Zip is
not installed. 7zr.exe is placed in the public domain by its author ("Igor Pavlov : Public domain").
